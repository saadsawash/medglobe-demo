01

Are you a shop?

No. MedGlobe is a company library - information about what we stand behind, not a cart for impulse buys. If something belongs here, it earned the shelf.

02

Do you carry everything that could sell?

No. We refuse volume for its own sake. A product has to clear the manufacturer, the evidence, the market need, and the people it is meant to help.
