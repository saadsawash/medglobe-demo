The line

Named for a person. Built for a period of care.

Yeshim is not a lifestyle fragrance line. It is a focused collection for skin that has become fragile during intensive treatment — formulated with pure plant oils, without synthetic perfume, parabens, or heavy chemical additives.

MedGlobe brings this story to markets where clinical trust and human care matter more than a shop grid. The Radika oncology collection is the source catalog; this library is how we present it.
