How it works

Counter-motion, not medication

A tuned mass damper sits on the back of the hand — the same idea used to quiet a tall building in an earthquake. It senses tremor frequency and answers with opposite motion.

  1. 01
    Sense

    The damper reads the tremor rhythm as you move through ordinary tasks.

  2. 02
    Answer

    Counter-motion reduces the shake so cups, pens, and utensils stay nearer to where they belong.

  3. 03
    Wear

    No battery. No overnight charge. Movement powers the response for a full day.
