FAQ

Common questions about Steadi-3 Plus

Answers for patients, caregivers, and healthcare providers evaluating assistive tremor technology.

Steadi-3 Plus is an assistive device for people with essential tremor and Parkinson's-related hand tremor. It is FDA-registered as a Class I medical device and intended to reduce hand tremor during daily activities.

No. Steadi-3 Plus is battery-free and powered by movement. The internal vibration disk responds mechanically to your tremor ù no charging routine required.

Once fitted and adjusted, the device calibrates to your tremor frequency automatically. Most users feel stabilization begin immediately after putting the glove on correctly.

Steadi-3 Plus is available for either hand with universal adjustable sizing. Some users wear one glove; others may use a device on each hand depending on clinical need and market availability.

In controlled evaluations, 84% of participants showed improved tremor control with Steadi-3 Plus versus no device, and 70% showed improvement over placebo based on blinded neurologist assessments. See the Evidence section for publication placeholders.
