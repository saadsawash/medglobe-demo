## Topics

1. Corporate homepage — corp-demo-1 selected; logo placement (left vs right on scroll)
2. City presence ribbon — real offices only; try scrolling and static versions
3. Typography — basic/standard fonts; Aptos; avoid antique or tiring display type
4. Product page — steadiwear-product-demo-4 layout chosen; demo-3 rejected
5. One product template for all SKUs — MedGlobe unity, not a marketplace of different designs
6. Medical / regulatory tone and color — borrow from corp-demo-1; more blue, not manufacturer logo colors
7. Yeshim (Radika) — second demo product; rejected recent label art; ~12 SKUs max; hospital story first
8. Markets — Steadiwear for Thailand, Israel, Turkey (not US); English first, translate later; Shopify locales
9. Reference — Saje Natural Wellness (fixed-left layout only; not shop-first hero)
10. Deliverables — refined corp-demo-1 + product-demo-4 by **Friday**; next call same time next week
