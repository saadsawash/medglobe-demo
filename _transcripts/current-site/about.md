**About MedGlobe USA**

MedGlobe USA delivers over 70 years of expertise in sourcing, distributing, and supporting medical instruments, topical formulations, and non-FDA devices worldwide. With a global presence spanning the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, we combine local market knowledge with seamless international logistics to meet the evolving needs of healthcare providers, distributors, and retailers.

**Global Presence, Local Expertise**

With offices across the Americas, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, MedGlobe combines in-market knowledge with global supply-chain capabilities. This structure allows us to respond quickly to customer needs and coordinate seamlessly across time zones and languages, ensuring a smooth experience for partners and clients worldwide.

**Decades of Experience**

Our leadership team brings deep expertise in product sourcing, regulatory affairs, and international logistics. Collectively, we have over 70 years of experience supporting manufacturers, healthcare providers, and retailers with reliable, transparent, and compliant solutions.

**Who We Serve**

MedGlobe works directly with distributors, retailers, clinics, laboratories, pharmacies, and other healthcare organizations seeking access to high-quality medical instruments, topical formulations, and non-FDA devices. Our onboarding process is straightforward, transparent, and designed to integrate smoothly with your operations.

**Our Approach**

We focus on building trusted, long-term partnerships by providing:

- Reliable sourcing of medical and dental instruments, topical creams, and non-FDA devices.
- Regulatory guidance for market-appropriate labeling, documentation, and compliance.
- Global logistics solutions, including warehousing, cross-border shipping, and last-mile delivery.
- Private label and co-branding support, from formulation sourcing to packaging coordination.
