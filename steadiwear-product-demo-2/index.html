Inside the object

Counter-motion, not medication

A tuned mass damper - the same principle used to quiet a tall building in an earthquake - rides on the back of the hand. It senses tremor frequency and answers with opposite motion, so the cup, the pen, and the fork stay nearer to where they belong.
